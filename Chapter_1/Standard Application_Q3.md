
**8) Standard Application:** A wooden cube with its outer faces painted is sawed up into 1000 smaller little cubes, all of the exact same size. The little cubes are then mixed up, and [one] is chosen at random. Determine the pr. [probability] of the chosen cube having exactly 2 painted faces.

$$\underline{\text{Soln}}$$

There are 10 cubes along 1 edge.

$$= 10 \times 10 \times 10 = 1000$$

The number of cubes that fit the description (Specific favorable outcomes $= 12 \times 8$). Because there are 8 cubes on the edge that fit the— the only cubes that fit the description specifically (exactly) are 8 cubes on the edge and there are 12 edges.

$$\text{Sample Space} = 1000$$

$$\therefore \text{Probability of the chosen having 2-painted faces} = \frac{\text{number of possible favorable outcomes}}{\text{Sample Space}}$$

$$= \frac{12 \times 8}{1000} = 0.096$$
