## Problem

Define the probability $P(A)$ of an event $A$ in the context of an experiment with a finite number of mutually exclusive, equiprobable outcomes. What two values are required to calculate this classical probability

## Solution

The two values required are the correct count of "favourable outcomes" and the correct count of "sample space".
